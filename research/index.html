Tools

EasyMeta lets you run your own meta-analysis online. To stress-test a paper before referees do, use our MAD app: four AI models read it in different roles and cross-examine each other. For Claude Code, our paper-workshop has referees from rival traditions argue over the paper and then rebuild it. All our tools, including a pre-review for ERC proposals, are on one page.
